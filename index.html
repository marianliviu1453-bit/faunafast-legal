Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026 · Last updated: July 25, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the FaunaFast Android application and this support website handle information. It applies to the current public release of FaunaFast.

2. Information stored by the app

FaunaFast may store information you enter or generate while using the app, including:

  • fasting start and end times, durations, and completion history;
  • fasting goals and session notes or choices, where available;
  • onboarding, appearance, haptic, and notification preferences;
  • local educational progress, including Trail and Bestiary state.

This information is stored locally on your Android device. FaunaFast does not require an account and does not transmit this information to the developer or to a FaunaFast server.

3. Information we do not collect through the app

The current release does not collect or share:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data;
  • advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data;
  • Health Connect, body-sensor, medical-record, or wearable data;
  • analytics, crash-reporting, or profiling data.

4. Android permissions

FaunaFast requests only permissions used for local app functions:

  • Notifications: optional fasting reminders and status notifications you enable.
  • Receive boot completed: restores eligible scheduled reminders after a restart or app update.
  • Vibration: optional haptic feedback and notification vibration.

These permissions are not used to collect, sell, or share personal data. Notification access can be changed in Android settings.

5. Backup, transfer, and deletion

FaunaFast disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its app data. Your data may therefore be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its storage, reset or replace your device, or lose access to it.

You can remove records through any deletion controls available in the app. To remove all FaunaFast data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

6. Data sharing and sale

FaunaFast does not sell personal data. The app does not share your locally stored fasting information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

7. Support communications

When you email support, we receive the email address, message content, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. We use this information only to respond, troubleshoot, maintain security, and meet applicable legal obligations. Do not send medical records or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is processed through the email services used by you and by the developer. It is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

8. This website

This website does not include FaunaFast analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. It is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process limited technical information, such as connection and security logs, under GitHub's own privacy terms.

9. Security

FaunaFast relies on Android's application storage protections and your device's security controls. No method of storage is completely secure, so you should protect your device with current software and an appropriate screen lock.

10. Children's privacy

FaunaFast does not knowingly collect app data from children or from any other users. A person below the age required to consent to email processing in their country should contact support only with permission from a parent or legal guardian.

11. Your choices and rights

Because app records remain on your device, the developer cannot access, export, correct, or delete them remotely. You control those records through the app and Android settings. For requests concerning support correspondence, contact us using the address below.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when FaunaFast's features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Health Disclaimer

FaunaFast is a general wellness and educational tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services. Information about fasting stages, animals, or cellular processes is educational and should not be treated as a personalized prediction of what is happening in your body.

Fasting may be inappropriate or unsafe for some people. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional before fasting if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have diabetes or another medical condition, take medication, have a history of an eating disorder, or have any concern about whether fasting is safe for you. Stop fasting and seek appropriate medical help if you feel unwell.
